    Meta-Evolve Architecture¶

    Status¶

    This owns shipped domain invariants. The roadmap owns scope; current status distinguishes available capabilities from future work under Design. Distributed execution defaults to inline calls; explicit process backends execute work on admitted trusted workers. External SDKs remain user-owned callables at the structural proposer seam.

    The idea¶

    Meta-Evolve evaluates proposed successors to immutable, versioned artifacts. The domain meaning does not depend on an artifact kind, model provider, search preset, evaluator implementation, or storage backend.

    Task -> Seed -> Proposal -> Successor -> Evaluation -> Search decision
                      \-> typed failure

    The public callable path hides kernel ceremony. Durable records retain the facts needed for lineage, accounting, inspection, comparison, and replay.

    Architectural layers¶

    Public API       improve, Task, artifacts, Session, run/resume, result queries
Application      normalization, coordination, comparison, replay
Domain           immutable identities, outcomes, evidence, budgets
Ports            proposer, evaluator, experience, workspace, storage
Adapters         callables, SDK agents, subprocesses, files, SQLite

    Dependencies point inward. Adapters translate external systems at the edge; the coordinator never branches on a provider, artifact type, evaluator, policy, or infrastructure implementation.

    Identities and lineage¶

    Content identity and occurrence identity are distinct. Equal artifact bytes may appear in different causal positions and therefore retain different occurrence IDs and parent links. Task, run, proposal, trial, evaluation, evidence, decision, and component identities are not collapsed for storage convenience.

    Every successor names its parents. Every decision-relevant observation is recorded at a logical time. Durable state is a projection of immutable records, not a mutable branch, workspace, cache, or provider conversation.

    Authority boundaries¶

    Candidate-controlled fields contain only proposed artifact content and declared derivation. Experiment-controlled fields include tasks, evaluators, data splits, seeds, environments, and budgets. Governance controls permissions, admission, visibility, and promotion.

    Proposal and promotion are separate¶

    Search may select a best measured artifact. That selection grants no production authority. A candidate cannot widen its budget, alter hidden evaluation, erase failures, change permissions, or promote itself. Future typed mutation surfaces must preserve this invariant; see the M8 design.

    Evaluation and failures¶

    Evaluation is independent of candidate claims and carries measurements, uncertainty, evidence, environment identity, and exact usage. Invalid output, proposer failure, evaluator failure, timeout, exhaustion, policy violation, and infrastructure failure remain distinct. An outcome without a valid measurement is non-rankable.

    Budgets and randomness¶

    Execution overrides inherit task ceilings and reject explicit widening. Start preparation resolves one effective budget before search binding or commitment; it determines identity and admission, and resume consumes the recorded value. The programming model owns the authoring contract. The coordinator owns seed/successor counts. Author Resources reports convert at existing result-to-record boundaries into count-free Usage before those counts are added; durable accounting shapes are unchanged. Unknown billed spend stays unknown through aggregation and replay; estimates stay evidence. Unverifiable monetary ceilings stop further work while retaining completed results. Experience access has separate bounds. Named random streams and recorded decisions make local trajectories replayable. External providers are reproducible to their transcript and declarations, without guaranteed byte-identical regeneration.

    Experience¶

    Experience is explicit and off by default. Pushed context is policy-selected, bounded, and committed before a proposal; pulled experience is capability-bounded and audited. Declared derivation may cite only authorized, strictly prior observations in recorded order. ProposerContext.evidence reads only the committed selection, with enabled state from the policy declaration. RecentAncestors@1 retains the newest accessible ancestor records; AncestorsOnly@1 retains the oldest. Both preserve canonical order and distinct record/rendering bounds. Lookup neither infers producer authority nor widens access; see the selected-evidence contract. M8.7.3 listing and reads authorize exact prior same-task/run artifact occurrences before a read-only storage edge revalidates and decodes source-tree / utf8-tree-v1; M8.7.4 applies that edge independently to both operands of a canonical library-owned diff. M8.7.5 composes those audited outcomes into one committed Git-neutral manifest, then projects it into disposable external Git metadata for a scoped workspace session. Invalid targets share one opaque denial; missing paths deny atomically. Whole-entry results use cumulative bounds and audit both exhaustion causes. Folding validates committed shape and the mount-owned operation prefix; durable loading checks stored bytes and reconstructs source results and the manifest; replay does no live read. Only successful source observations grant derivation authority. Whole-tree reader operations, semantic retrieval, persistent Git identity, and cross-run access remain unshipped under the source-inspection contract.

    Workspaces and external execution¶

    Repository proposers receive a private mutable workspace from an immutable artifact; successful capture creates a new immutable occurrence, and cleanup precedes commitment. Evaluators own fresh workspaces, commands, environments, hidden graders, and failure translation.

    Durability and replay¶

    Artifacts and append-only transitions are durable truth. SQLite and filesystem stores preserve committed facts; caches and worker liveness do not. Writes validate each new batch against ephemeral grammar state for the committed prefix. That state and the projection advance only after a successful atomic commit with the expected version. Reopening still validates the full history using the same grammar rules; no cache is persisted or trusted across processes. Reopen is a read-only projection, and a read-only storage handle observes live WAL commits with no write, migration, or execution authority. open_run() owns that handle for a context block, validates one explicit or sole run, and yields a live Run; lexical identities never imply recency. It adds no durable records or execution authority. SQLite may maintain WAL sidecars without changing committed history. Resume first verifies component manifests and the recovery frontier, then performs only missing logical work. Replay never reruns a proposer, evaluator, context selector, or provider. Distributed retries may execute an uncommitted operation again while an abandoned call still runs. Lease and epoch checks enforce at-most-once acceptance; content-derived run IDs never couple execution in independent stores. Run.inspect() derives one immutable aggregate from one replay. Registered proposer manifests cover user source, invocation, evidence views/errors, context construction, and adapter wiring; context-policy manifests cover the factory and selection/rendering module. Incompatible source refuses resume before runner construction, preflight, or work; supported histories stay readable. See the callback contract. Session fixes authority and budget; each step advances the classified frontier to ready or stopped without asking search twice. Lifecycle means Stop, rankability means successful evaluation, and bootstrap remains a trial. Summary and inspection derive lifecycle, rankability, and committed usage from one validated frontier each, including a pending distributed seed evaluation. Recorded usage is independent of policy readiness; failures retain their costs and pending work contributes no estimated charge. Summaries are nondurable views with an optional score; baseline and distinct typed failure counts share the captured frontier, while winner queries and comparisons remain strict. Capture-handle persistence and recorded capabilities do not authorize resume or change durable identity. ArchiveUpdate.member_ids is the authoritative ordered membership; an admission may remove any number of members, and evicted_artifact_id names exactly one or is None. Specialists is a GEPA-style per-key frontier, not a Pareto archive: it derives one winner per configured metric from committed evaluations and samples a metric uniformly to expand that metric's winner, so ownership over more metrics means more selection weight. TreeSearch is an ordinary tree-search@1 SearchProgram, not an archive: a private immutable fold derives one node per materialized artifact occurrence, its visits, and its best-descendant subtree Q entirely from durable trial and evaluation facts, then widens while child_count < ceil(sqrt(node_visits)) or otherwise descends by normalized UCT.

    Safe exact-kind presentation reads frozen payloads into bounded inert records; decoded values are never inspected and canonical attachments remain exact. Captured run reports render bounded scalars as text or escaped HTML without storage, codec, component, or decoded-artifact calls. The loopback browser keeps storage on one thread and artifact routes within one snapshot; cached payloads add bounded-LRU memory without a byte copy.

    Replaceability¶

    Introduce a port only when a current acceptance test needs a second implementation. Published conformance suites cover codecs, repository proposers, evaluators, search programs, context policies, workspaces, sandboxes, and typed mutation surfaces; store contracts remain under tests/. Each suite states which laws it does not check.

    Compatibility anchors¶

    Improve¶

    improve() is declaration sugar with no independent durable identity or execution semantics. It constructs Task, bounded Greedy, and Experiment, then enters ordinary local run(). Its objective becomes Task.objectives=(objective,) or inherits the default; context uses the existing Experiment.context boundary without pull access. Equivalent declarations retain identical identity and committed facts at the same revision; handle-specific summary metadata can differ across stores. Its storage may persist local history without implying durable-local reconstruction or resumability.

    Experiment¶

    Experiment is an immutable declaration value, not a new durable record or serialization identity. Positional typed declarations and the source-compatible keyword run() form normalize before validation and enter the same execution path. Storage, mode, and the narrowing call-level budget remain execution overlays. Equality is dataclass equality over the eight fields; it does not promise structural equality for live proposer or registry objects.

    Functional search¶

    @search is a dependency-free adapter over the ordinary SearchProgram boundary, not a second coordinator path. Its callback chooses one opaque seed or incumbent occurrence handle, or stops with a validated reason. A private shared lowerer applies the same envelope and authorized unit trial construction as Greedy; the two retain distinct truthful policy and run identities. Complete immutable decorator configuration flows into SearchSpec and run identity. Durable-local reconstruction remains explicit registration through the existing policy-factory extension. Multiple parents, archives, and population-shaped actions stay on SearchProgram.next().

    Programming harnesses¶

    HarnessArtifact is the built-in agent-harness domain using harness-manifest-v1. Its v1 schema contains a planner TextParam and a context-policy PolicyParam; their names, component references, interfaces, and surface types are immutable across a successor. Only text or policy configuration may change.

    Artifact codecs may structurally expose validate_successor(parents, candidate). Proposal completion invokes it after candidate encoding and before artifact creation. Codecs without the hook retain their behavior. This generic boundary is core-owned, so an honest wrapper may use MutationSurface.apply() while a bypassing wrapper receives the same independent check.

    Harness search evaluates development episodes only. Held-out episodes run once for the unchanged baseline and once for the selected harness as seed-only Greedy(max_trials=0) runs. Strict comparison requires equal evaluator, artifact, objective, budget, proposer, search, randomness, context, experience, and immutable harness declarations. It reports promotion eligibility but owns no promotion or deployment authority.

    Portable repository episodes digest public source, hidden evaluator tests, process controls, and explicit development/held-out splits. The example-owned evaluator launches fresh isolated inner runs and records complete captured attempts. Resume guarantees begin at a committed outer evaluation; uncommitted inner work may repeat.

    The research harness example owns its source-view and citation conventions: evaluator-owned views are evidence, not artifact lineage or selected exports, and usable citations require completed opens in first-exposure order. Those example restrictions supplement the kernel observation ledger without adding authority.

    Nested improvement loops¶

    PolicyArtifact is the built-in search-policy domain using policy-manifest-v1. Its only mutable values are parent selection (seed or incumbent) and a non-negative voluntary trial limit. Fixed schema-owned names, component declarations, interfaces, allowed domains, tasks, seeds, proposers, evaluators, environments, budgets, split membership, caching, aggregation, and promotion remain outside candidate authority.

    CandidatePolicy is the trusted candidate-policy@1 interpreter registered through the ordinary policy registry. Its canonical artifact payload is the search configuration and therefore part of the existing RunId; durable-local reconstruction and resume require no coordinator branch.

    Policy episodes are user-owned composition around ordinary runs. Each isolated inner run retains its own events, lineage, evidence, failures, usage, RunId, and evaluator-owned storage location. The existing content-addressed RunId is the sole cache identity. Development may reopen an exact completed run or resume a compatible incomplete durable run. Held-out execution always starts in a new empty namespace. Aggregates are recomputed from outcomes rather than cached separately.

    Any failed, incomplete, or missing episode makes an evaluation non-rankable while preserving partial outcomes. Inner trial/evaluation counts remain inner; only token, monetary-spend, and elapsed usage rolls into an enclosing evaluation. Separate seed-only held-out outer runs compare baseline and selected candidate under equal external controls. Comparison reports promotion eligibility without granting promotion authority. See the policy-evolution guide.

    Boundaries with adjacent systems¶

    Research influences are in the research catalog and do not override shipped semantics; a store's trust boundary is durable trust.

    Invariants¶

    • Artifacts are immutable; equal content does not collapse causal occurrences.
    • Parent lineage, component identity, randomness, evidence, and usage are explicit.
    • Candidates cannot control evaluation, budgets, permissions, or promotion.
    • Failures without valid measurements are never ranked as low scores.
    • Replay projects committed facts without external execution.
    • Replaceable components enter through ports, never coordinator special cases.
